# Phase 2a user guide: signing in

1. Open the web app. Sign in with your **username (or email) and password**. In the local demo you can also click **Continue as demo user**, or pick another demo account (Finance, HR, Site…) from the list.
2. **Wrong password?** You see the same message whatever went wrong, so nobody can find out which usernames exist. After **5 wrong attempts in a row** the account is locked for **15 minutes**.
3. **First sign-in, or after a reset:** you must choose your own password before anything else opens. The page shows the rules as you type:
   - at least 12 characters (a short sentence is easiest to remember);
   - not a common password;
   - no repeated characters or simple sequences like "aaaa" or "1234";
   - not containing your name, username or email.
4. **Change your password** any time from the account menu (top right). Your other browsers and phones are signed out when you do.
5. **Staying signed in:** the web app signs you out after **30 minutes without activity**, and after **12 hours** in any case. Two minutes before, a box asks **Are you still there?**; click **Stay signed in** to carry on. The phone app (from Phase 2c) allows 7 days offline.
6. **Leavers:** when the admin deactivates someone (Phase 2c screen), they are signed out everywhere at once and can no longer sign in.
7. **Sign out** from the account menu. On a shared computer, always sign out.
